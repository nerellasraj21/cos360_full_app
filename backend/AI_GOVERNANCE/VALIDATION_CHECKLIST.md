# AI Output Validation Checklist

This checklist is a **hard gate** before acceptance, merge, or deployment.

---

## Evidence Verification

- [ ] All claims are supported by evidence
- [ ] No invented files, APIs, or configurations

---

## Assumption Disclosure

- [ ] All assumptions are explicitly listed
- [ ] No silent or implied assumptions

---

## Scope Control

- [ ] No scope creep
- [ ] No unrelated changes

---

## Reproducibility

- [ ] Bug reproduced OR explicitly stated as non-reproducible
- [ ] Feature validated against defined success criteria

---

## SOP Compliance

- [ ] Output labels present
- [ ] Handover document exists
- [ ] Confidence level declared

---

## Final Decision

- [ ] ACCEPT
- [ ] REJECT
